# PI Source Research

Use this skill when researching communities, archives, forums, source providers,
industries or acquisition strategies.

Rules:
- Prefer practitioner and business communities over founder-launch communities.
- Evaluate source quality by useful evidence yield, not community size.
- Track strong signals per 1000 items, payment signals per 1000 items and
  acquisition cost/effort where measurable.
- Preserve source item IDs, timestamps, URLs/permalinks and provider provenance.
- Treat access status and source lifecycle explicitly.
- Do not assume official Reddit API/OAuth is available.
- Do not make paid social-listening/data providers a required production dependency.
- Do not bypass authentication, robots restrictions, CAPTCHAs, private endpoints,
  access controls or rate limits.
- Third-party archives must remain isolated behind source/provider abstractions.
- Sensitive domains should focus on professional workflow, software, process and
  administrative problems rather than sensitive individual case data.

Distinguish:
technical availability,
policy/usage-rights status,
data completeness,
evidence quality,
and production suitability.

A technically working endpoint is not automatically an approved production source.
