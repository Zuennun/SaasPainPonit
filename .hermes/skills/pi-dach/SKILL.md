# PI DACH Validation

Never treat global evidence as automatically representative of Germany, Austria
or Switzerland.

Keep Global Evidence and DACH Evidence separate.

For a candidate problem evaluate:
- actor/workflow equivalence,
- whether the same job actually exists in DACH,
- local evidence,
- local direct and indirect competitors,
- current manual workaround,
- user,
- buyer,
- decision maker,
- gatekeeper,
- procurement friction,
- switching costs,
- required local integrations,
- regulation only when relevant,
- localization gap.

Relevant integrations may include systems such as DATEV, Lexware, ELSTER or SAP
when the actual workflow requires them.

Do not add regulatory complexity merely because the market is DACH.

Explicitly state when DACH evidence is weak or absent.
