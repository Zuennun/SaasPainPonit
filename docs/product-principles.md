# Product principles

This file is the compact implementation authority derived from the product brief.
Changes to these rules require an explicit product decision.

1. Never invent evidence. Unknown values remain `null`.
2. Every factual opportunity claim is linked to one or more evidence spans.
3. Global evidence and DACH evidence are stored and presented separately.
4. A strong single signal is allowed, but is labelled as a single signal.
5. Evidence states describe research maturity, not business quality.
6. Payment evidence is observable behaviour, never an inferred willingness score.
7. Competition and counter-evidence research are required before completion.
8. Incidents, support questions, and user errors do not automatically become opportunities.
9. Sources and AI providers are replaceable at the domain boundary.
10. The final output recommends validation questions, not building a product.

Decision priority: evidence integrity, research quality, user trust, correctness,
auditability, maintainability, cost, performance, polish, feature quantity.
